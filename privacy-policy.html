Privacy Policy

App Name: Spark - Daily Book Summaries (com.gulab.spark)
Developer: Vinsona
Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Vinsona ("we," "our," or "us") built the Spark - Daily Book Summaries mobile application as an ad-supported educational and self-improvement app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized reading and audio summary experience across devices, we collect minimal data:

  • Account & Sync Identifiers: If you choose to sign in using your Google Email address, we store your email address to sync your daily reading progress across devices. If you skip sign-in, a random, non-personal Device ID is generated to save your reading progress locally and in our cloud database.
  • App Preferences & Progress: We store your selected language preference (English or Hinglish), completed days, and unlocked archive days.
  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Our third-party advertising and analytics partners automatically collect device information such as Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device model, and OS version to serve relevant ads and measure app performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver daily book summaries and stream audio narrations.
  • To save and restore your reading progress and unlocked content.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob to keep the app free for users.
  • To monitor app stability, fix crashes, and improve user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Please review their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Analytics)

4. Data Security & Retention

Your reading progress and email identifier (if provided) are securely stored on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your synced email address and reading progress data at any time by contacting us via email. Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your user record from our database within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
