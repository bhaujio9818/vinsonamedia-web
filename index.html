How Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) Work

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows individuals to invest small, fixed amounts of money periodically into mutual funds or market instruments. Through rupee-cost averaging and compounding growth, long-term investors benefit significantly over market volatility.

The Compounding Formula

M = P × ({[1 + i]^n - 1} / i) × (1 + i)

Where M is total returns, P is monthly deposit, i is monthly interest rate, and n is total months.

Understanding Equated Monthly Installments (EMI)

An EMI is a fixed monthly payment provided by a borrower to a lender on a specified date. Each payment includes components of both principal repayment and interest charges. Using a standard reducing-balance method, the interest portion gradually decreases as the principal is paid down.
